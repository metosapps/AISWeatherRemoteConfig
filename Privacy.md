# Weather Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to Weather, package name `app.weather.usa.forecast.galaxy2023p`.

Weather is designed to be privacy-friendly. The app does not require an account and does not sell personal data.

## Data We Use

- Approximate or precise location, only when you grant Android location permission.
- Saved city names and coordinates, stored locally on your device.
- Weather settings such as units, theme, refresh interval, language, notification preference, analytics preference, and wallpaper style.
- Basic anonymous app events when usage analytics is enabled.
- Advertising and device information processed by advertising SDKs when ads are enabled.
- Purchase status for Premium products, handled through Samsung In-App Purchase.

## How Location Is Used

Location is used only to request weather for your current area while you use the app. Weather does not request background location access.

## Weather Data

Weather data is provided by Open-Meteo through the Weather proxy. The proxy normalizes and caches weather, air quality, and city search responses to improve reliability and reduce direct API usage.

## Radar and Map Data

Radar and map views may use RainViewer, Carto, and OpenStreetMap data. These providers may receive standard network request information required to load map and radar tiles.

## Usage Analytics

Weather can send basic anonymous app events to Firebase Analytics and the Weather proxy to help improve reliability and product decisions. Events may include screen names, app version, Android SDK version, setting changes, weather refresh events, and broad weather codes.

Analytics events do not include account data, contacts, city names, precise coordinates, or advertising identifiers. You can turn usage analytics off from Settings.

## Advertising

The app can show ads through remote configuration. The Galaxy Store build is configured for App Open, Rewarded Video, and Rewarded Interstitial ads. Banner and native ad placements are disabled. Ad providers may process advertising-related data according to their own policies and any required consent flow. Premium users should not see ads in production builds.

## Premium Purchases

Weather may offer Premium products such as `premium_monthly`, `premium_yearly`, and `premium_lifetime` through Samsung In-App Purchase. Samsung processes payment information. Weather uses purchase status only to unlock Premium features such as themes, wallpapers, widgets, and the premium ad-free promise.

## Accounts

No account is required. Weather does not provide account creation, so there is no account deletion flow.

## Data Sharing

Weather does not sell user data. Weather requests are sent only as needed to provide forecasts. Analytics, ads, payment, and infrastructure data may be processed by Firebase, Google AdMob, Samsung In-App Purchase, Cloudflare, and related service providers as described above.

## Data Retention

Saved cities and settings remain on your device until you change them, clear app data, or uninstall the app. Server logs and analytics are retained only as needed for reliability, abuse prevention, and product improvement.

## Security

Weather uses HTTPS for network requests. Local app settings and saved cities are stored on your Android device using platform storage.
